The Pitch

Group Entry lets one player enter a contest and invite specific friends to buy in and own a share of that same entry — all directly on Splash. Ownership and payouts are handled natively on the platform, with zero platform fees on the split.

The one rule to remember

Splitting only happens before the contest's first pick locks. Group Entry is a pre-contest setup tool — it can never be started or edited once the contest is underway.
