Entrants Manager Basics

The 30-second version: Entrants Manager is the commissioner's roster hub, inside the Commissioner Console, alongside Email entrants and Pick log. It's where they track who's in, who's paid, who's missing picks, and where they can message, pause, or remove entrants. A large share of commissioner tickets trace back to this one screen — here's how it actually works.

Entrants tab

The searchable roster — filter, mark paid, add notes, export, and take bulk action.

Email entrants

Message all entrants or just the ones missing picks, from the commissioner's name or handle.

Pick log

Audit trail of already-locked picks by week/slate — never live or future picks.

Manage entries

Per-entry drill-down — weekly Make Picks buttons, and independent Pause/Remove per entry.

Bulk select

On the Entrants tab, filter to Missing picks, check the boxes for the entrants who need a nudge, then click Email (N) from the action bar — it jumps to Email entrants with those recipients pre-filled.

Email entrants tab

Skip the selection step entirely — go straight to Email entrants and choose the Missing picks (N) recipient group, pick a sender identity (full name or Splash handle), write a message, and send.

Don't tell commissioners Remove is reversible

Pause freezes an entrant or entry without deleting history and can be reversed. Remove takes them out of the contest entirely and is the more permanent action — if a commissioner isn't sure whether someone's coming back, steer them to Pause first.

The one rule that matters most

Pause is reversible. Remove is not. When a commissioner isn't sure whether an entrant is coming back — late payment, temporary no-show, anything uncertain — the safe default answer from CS is always Pause, not Remove.
