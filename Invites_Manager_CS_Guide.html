Invites Manager Basics

The 30-second version: Invites Manager is where a commissioner brings people into their contest — inside the Commissioner Console, right next to Entrants Manager. It has two tabs, New invite and Sent, and four distinct ways to build an invite list. A contest is only as good as its roster, so this is one of the highest-leverage screens a commissioner touches before a contest locks.

Share link

A public join link to post anywhere — group chat, social, email.

Import emails

Paste a known list of contacts, up to 250 per import.

Previous Splash entrants

Pull in returning players from the commissioner's past contests.

Followers

Select from people who already follow the commissioner on Splash.

Share link (in-flow)

From "Add to invite," clicking Share link displays the join URL directly on the page, with its own Copy invite link button right below it.

Copy invite link (shortcut)

A standalone Copy invite link button sits in the top-right corner of the whole Invites Manager page — same link, one click, no need to open the people-picker flow at all.

Don't assume last year's roster carries over

Splash never auto-migrates entrants between contests, even for a repeat annual pool with the same commissioner and group. If a commissioner asks why their old members aren't showing up, the answer is always: they need a fresh invite via Previous Splash entrants.

The one rule that matters most

The invite link is public and uncapped by Invites Manager itself — anyone who has it can join, up to whatever max-entries limit the contest is set to. It is not a private, per-person list the way Import emails or Previous Splash entrants are.
