Overview

What Is Invites Manager & Why It Matters

The Invites Manager is where a commissioner brings people into the contest. It lives in the left-hand Commissioner Console sub-navigation, right next to Entrants Manager, and is organized into two tabs: New invite and Sent.

Why this matters to commissioners A contest is only as good as its roster. Whether a commissioner is filling out a fresh contest before it locks, or re-inviting people who played last season, Invites Manager is the single place that covers every way to reach them — a public link, known emails, past Splash entrants, or their own followers — without leaving Splash or juggling a separate contact list.
Tip Splash doesn't automatically carry entrants over between contests. Every new contest needs fresh invitations, even for people who played last time — that's exactly what the Previous Splash entrants option is built for.

New Invite

Four Ways to Add People

The New invite tab's "Add to invite" panel offers four distinct ways to build an invite list, each suited to a different audience.

New invite — Share link, Import emails, Previous Splash entrants, and Followers

OptionBest for
Share linkA single link to post anywhere — group chat, social, email — for anyone to join with
Import emailsA known list of contacts, pasted or typed directly
Previous Splash entrantsReturning players from past contests the commissioner has run
FollowersPeople who already follow the commissioner on Splash

Import emails

Paste or type emails, separated by a comma and/or space — duplicates are automatically skipped

The box supports up to 250 emails per import. Commissioners who need more can request VIP status; the invite link itself has no such limit and can always be shared without restriction.

Previous Splash entrants

A searchable, paginated list of the commissioner's past contests — select one or more to pull in their entrants

Each row shows the contest name, date, format, and a clickable entrant count. Checking a contest's box adds its entrants to the invite list — this is the main path for re-inviting an entire group who played together before.

Followers

Search and select from people who follow the commissioner on Splash

Tip These four methods aren't exclusive — a commissioner can mix and match (e.g. import a few known emails, then add an entire past contest's entrants) before reviewing and sending the combined invite list.

Quick Share

Copy Invite Link

In the top-right corner of Invites Manager sits a standalone Copy invite link button — a shortcut for commissioners who just want the join link without going through the people-picker flow.

Copy invite link — grabs the same link shown under Share link, ready to paste anywhere

This is the same link available under the Share link option in "Add to invite" — it's just surfaced as a one-click shortcut for speed. Anyone with the link can join the contest directly, up to the contest's max entries.

Note This is a public join link. Anyone who has it can enter the contest — it isn't restricted to a specific list of people the way Import emails or Previous Splash entrants are.

Review & Send

The Invite Panel

Everyone added through any of the four methods lands in the Invite panel on the right, where the commissioner reviews the list before sending.

Invite (3) — search, remove individuals or clear the whole list, add a custom message, then send

  • A live count (Invite (N)) and an All filter/search box for the growing list
  • A trash icon to clear the entire list in one action
  • An x next to each person to remove them individually
  • An optional Add a custom message to the invite checkbox, for a personal note alongside the standard invite
  • Send invite — delivers to everyone currently in the list
Warn The trash icon clears everyone in the list at once — there's no per-batch undo, so double-check before using it if the list combines people pulled in from multiple sources.

Tracking

The Sent Tab

The Sent tab tracks everyone who has already been invited — a running record so the commissioner (or CS, when troubleshooting) can confirm whether an invite actually went out, separate from the New invite tab's in-progress list.

Tip If someone says they never received an invite, checking the Sent tab is the first step — it confirms whether the invite was actually sent before assuming a delivery issue.

FAQ

Do returning entrants need a new invite every season?

Yes. Splash doesn't carry entrants over between contests automatically — every new contest needs fresh invitations, even for people who played last time. Previous Splash entrants is built specifically for this.

What's the difference between the top-right Copy invite link and the Share link option?

They're the same link. Copy invite link is just a one-click shortcut in the header so a commissioner doesn't have to open the people-picker flow if all they want is the join link.

Is there a limit on how many people can join via the invite link?

The link itself isn't capped by Invites Manager, but the contest's overall max entries (shown in the sidebar, e.g. "100 max") still applies — once full, no more entries can be added regardless of the invite method.

How many emails can be imported at once?

Up to 250 per import. Commissioners who regularly need more can request VIP status; duplicates within a paste are automatically skipped.

Can a commissioner combine multiple invite methods before sending?

Yes — Share link, Import emails, Previous Splash entrants, and Followers all feed into the same Invite panel, so a commissioner can mix sources before reviewing and sending one combined invite.

How can a commissioner tell if an invite actually went out?

Check the Sent tab — it's the record of everyone who has already been invited, separate from the in-progress New invite list.

What happens if a commissioner clicks the trash icon in the Invite panel?

It clears the entire in-progress invite list at once. There's no per-batch undo, so it's worth double-checking the list first, especially if it combines people from more than one source.
